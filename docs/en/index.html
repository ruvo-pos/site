Small things that save a shift

What happens mid-rush, handled in the system itself.

  • Courses and “Fire”

    The second course waits in the kitchen until the waiter fires it — then the ticket rings and flashes.

  • Stop list with portions

    A dish goes on stop by itself when its portions run out; nobody can order more than is left.

  • Split bills

    Pay by items or in parts, each part with its own payment method.

  • Change without mistakes

    Type what the guest handed over, and RUVO shows the change in big digits.

  • Cancelling with a PIN

    A sent dish is cancelled only by someone allowed to, with a reason; if the kitchen had started, the ingredients go to waste.

  • Discounts under control

    Staff discounts and complimentary dishes only with a manager’s PIN, and all of it stays in the action log.

  • “Bring the table back”

    A closed table reopens with a PIN and a reason; stock and sales correct themselves.

  • A floor plan in a minute

    Move tables, rooms and labels by dragging on the front terminal.

  • Personal PIN

    Everyone works under their own name, and the terminal locks itself after five minutes.

  • Delivery on the same screen

    Orders for Wolt and Glovo go to the kitchen together with the floor, with the courier’s arrival time.

  • A working day until 5 a.m.

    For places open after midnight: sales land on the right day.

  • Allergens for guests

    The 14 EU allergens for every dish, from its recipe, on the kiosk and for the waiter.

    Pricing questions

    What is included in the venue price?
    One front terminal and one kitchen screen, the menu, stock and recipes, reports with the AI assistant, staff and roles. Other devices and modules — by the price list.
    How do I pay?
    Monthly — an invoice to your company every month, or yearly — one invoice in advance, 10 months instead of 12. By bank transfer; prices are without VAT, and 20% VAT is added to the invoice.
    What if I add a device mid-year?
    A device or module added in the middle of a paid year is paid monthly until that year ends. On a monthly subscription — from the next invoice.
    Can I use my own devices?
    Yes. The front terminal and the kiosk run on iPad, the waiter device on iPhone, the kitchen screen on a tablet — the devices you already have will do.
    Does RUVO replace the fiscal register?
    No. Your register still issues the fiscal receipt, and RUVO records the payment method so the totals match.
    Is there a lock-in contract?
    No. End the subscription whenever you like, and we send you your data in a standard format.
    What if a payment is late?
    The floor and the kitchen keep working — we call before anything is switched off.
    How do I get support?
    Write to hello@ruvo.ac — we answer on working days.
