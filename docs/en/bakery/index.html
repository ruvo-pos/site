    Pricing questions

    How do I pay?

    Monthly — an invoice to your company every month, or yearly — one invoice in advance, 10 months instead of 12. By bank transfer; prices are without VAT, and 20% VAT is added to the invoice.

    What if I add a device mid-year?

    A device or module added in the middle of a paid year is paid monthly until that year ends. On a monthly subscription — from the next invoice.

    Can I use my own devices?

    Yes. The front terminal and the kiosk run on iPad, the waiter device on iPhone, the kitchen screen on a tablet — the devices you already have will do.

    Does RUVO replace the fiscal register?

    No. Your register still issues the fiscal receipt, and RUVO records the payment method so the totals match.

    Is there a lock-in contract?

    No. End the subscription whenever you like, and we send you your data in a standard format.

    What if a payment is late?

    The floor and the kitchen keep working — we call before anything is switched off.
